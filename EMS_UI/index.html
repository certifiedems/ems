Who it's for

Technicians and engineers working in electronics manufacturing services (EMS), SMT lines, control panels, PLCs and plant automation who want a recognised, verifiable proof of skill, from first job to technical leadership.

How certification works

  • Create an account and apply for your level.
  • Study the published syllabus for that level.
  • Book an exam slot and sit a remotely proctored online assessment.
  • Pass to receive a digital certificate with a verifiable credential.

Certification levels and syllabus

L1 Foundation

  • Electrical fundamentals
  • Power distribution basics
  • Protection and switching devices
  • Transformers and power supplies
  • Motors and starters
  • Sensors
  • Cables, panels and drawings
  • SMT foundation and IPC-A-610
  • Workplace and electrical safety
  • Basic mechanics and pneumatics

L2 Intermediate

  • Power quality and machines
  • Drives and motion control
  • Industrial instrumentation
  • PLC systems and programming
  • PLC troubleshooting
  • Industrial communications
  • Control fundamentals
  • SMT process engineering
  • Cables, EMI and panel engineering
  • Structured fault finding

L3 Advanced

  • Line qualification and process validation
  • Statistical process control and Six Sigma
  • Failure analysis and root cause
  • DFM / DFT leadership
  • New product introduction management
  • Advanced control, SCADA and data
  • Network architecture and OT security
  • Robotics and advanced automation
  • Power quality, energy and compliance
  • Audit, compliance and technical leadership
